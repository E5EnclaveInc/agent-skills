# Source Prompts — brand_sprint

Distilled from the GrokBot Galaxy 30-prompt set (Ship by Thursday build,
September 15–17, 2026). Provenance: prompts collected from the three builders'
posts and the "30 GROKBOT PROMPTS" card; full text archived with the original source set.

## B02 — Blank org, deadline brand
> Name the company around its shipping deadline, create the GitHub
> organization and write the initial product README.

## B05 — Company name → domain → email
> Generate 15 company names, then evaluate the best 5 by domain, email format
> and ability to work as both a company and product brand.

## A09 — Visual direction
> Generate the complete visual direction for the product: brand voice, hero
> copy, CTAs, colors and an implementation-ready design brief.

## C08 — Designer bot, 60-minute sprint
> Create a designer bot that delivers the company name, logo directions and
> landing-page hero within 60 minutes.

## Adaptation notes
- GrokBot's designer/naming "bots" → a bounded sprint run by agents with your
  team's design register; the timebox is the point, not the bot.
- The standing rule: taste decisions (names, marks, copy) are settled by
  deliberative consensus and never handed to the principal as a menu — the
  sprint ends with a decided identity, not options.
- Naming and identity provision sit OUTSIDE revenue-wedge and prototype-sprint
  (they were absorbed there in the first pass); this skill owns the full
  identity job. Revenue-wedge's deadline-brand pattern (B02/B05 as
  wedge-enablers) now points here.
