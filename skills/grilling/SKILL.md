# Grilling

> **Attribution:** Adapted from mattpocock/skills (MIT). Upstream is canonical; this adaptation adds E5's curation and promotion-gate framing.
>
> Method only; no code or text copied. See `LICENSE` for the upstream MIT license notice.

The reusable interview behind grill-me and grill-with-docs.

## The questions

1. What breaks if this is wrong? (blast radius)
2. What are you assuming that you have not verified? (assumptions)
3. What is the smallest version that still proves the idea? (slice)
4. What would make you abandon this approach? (kill criteria)
5. Who is affected and have they been heard? (stakeholders)

## Rules

- One question at a time; each answer is recorded before the next.
- "I don't know" is data — it becomes an open question, not a failure.
- The grilling ends with a verdict: proceed, reshape, or stop — stated
  plainly with reasons.
