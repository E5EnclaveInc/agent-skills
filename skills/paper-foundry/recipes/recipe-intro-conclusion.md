# Recipe: Introduction and Conclusion Architecture

Run this LAST, not first. The opening must earn the thesis (which recipe-contribution-claim already built); the closing must not introduce new claims. Drafting the intro first is how papers end up promising what they never deliver.

## Openings (pick one)

**Paradox opening** (L2-E2, L2-E14, L2-E13, L2-E10 — the history lane's signature): stage a contradiction in the first breath. Ubiquity plus unknowability. Revolution next door to entrenchment, fifty miles away. The master concept versus the scene it cannot see. The company archive versus the captives' worldview it never meant to record. The contradiction *is* the paper's reason to exist; the reader feels the gap before it is named. Do not resolve the paradox in the opening — inhabit it.

**Cold-open vignette** (L2-E13): open *in medias res* with a fully realized archival scene — vessel, date, death, quoted sailor's account, spatial arrangement ("concentric circles, innermost ring… youngest girls, elderly perimeter") — before any literature review or thesis statement. The analytic term ("soul of sociality") arrives smuggled inside the scene's own language. Evidence performs the argument before the argument is declared. Requires a scene strong enough to carry the concept; do not manufacture one.

**Inventory → pivot** (L1-E4): empirical inventory first (programs exist, proposals abound), then the pivot to the neglected dimension. Works for taxonomy and design papers: the inventory establishes authority; the pivot establishes the gap.

**Taxonomy opening** (L1-E13): name the competing procedures neutrally, then spend the paper adjudicating. The opening's neutrality is the credential — the reader trusts the judging because the setup didn't prejudge.

**Constraint-then-design** (L1-E3): name the political/discursive constraint explicitly (the post-racial climate; the race-neutrality requirement), then show the design that satisfies it. The constraint is the opening's tension; the design is its release.

**Problem-statement opening** (L1-E11): name the problems cleanly in parallel, no narrative. "Two problems: legal obstacles from the passage of time; economic difficulties in realistic present-value estimation." Fastest opening in the corpus. Use when the problems are genuinely crisp.

**Diagnosis-first** (L1-E1): stark intergenerational assessment up front (the wealth gap as present fact), mechanism second. Present → deep past → forward program. The arc is the argument.

**Definitional opening** (L1-E8): start from shared comparative ground (reparations programs "in general" aim at acknowledgment, redress, closure), then particularize. Files the paper inside an established literature instead of inventing a frame.

**Origin-story opening** (L1-E5): why the field exists — the standard explanations were defamatory, so the field is the response. Past-justification as opening; the extension list (where the field goes) becomes the close.

**Adversarial tableau** (L1-E6): stage the three-cornered fight (opponents' frames named and attributed), then let the data adjudicate. The tableau's fairness is the credential — mirror the opponents' frames accurately ("Justice and Greed") or the adjudication reads as rigged.

**Hourglass roadmap** (L1-E3, full text): prior-result gap → historical narrative of the mechanism → theoretical grounding → data/methods roadmap → results preview → contribution placement. Wide (literature) → narrow (specification) → wide (meaning). Preview all four evidentiary stages in the intro; the reader climbs a staircase they have already seen.

**Infrastructural opening** (L2-E10, per review): begin on logistics and seasonality (the trade's calendar) before widening to the human story. An infrastructural opening for a humanistic argument — the material base first, the meaning second.

## Closings (pick one; must match the opening's promise)

**Cumulative aggregation** (L1-E1): the eras "taken collectively" as a closing amplifier. Each block sufficed alone; together the case is impossible to ignore. Only works if the blocks are genuinely modular (recipe-evidence-weave).

**Constructive exit** (L1-E3, L1-E8): end on the proposal or the valuation anchor — the number's foundation, not the moral case. The paper exits forward, into policy.

**Procedural deferral** (L1-E9, L1-E11): hand the final verdict to politics — "to be determined by negotiations"; "political rather than narrowly legal processes will determine any ultimate claims." A humility move and a scope delimiter in one: the paper claims the menu, not the meal.

**Universal generalization** (L1-E10): end not on the recipient group but on the population — the remedy benefits everyone. Particular diagnosis → universal prescription. Reframes the entire paper in the last paragraph; use deliberately.

**Research-agenda extension** (L1-E5): the closing move doubles as a claim on future territory (multiple identities, "passing," wealth sources, mobility). The survey ends by drawing the map it will not itself explore.

**Causal verdict** (L1-E12): data → discontinuity → verdict. The standard empirical close: restate what the design showed, in causal language, with no new claims.

**Canon-revision close** (L2-E9): establish the event inside the lineage it was excluded from (Haiti inside democracy/human rights). The closing re-files history.

**Disciplined refusal of closure** (L2-E2): do not resolve the paradox; inhabit the limit — describe "as fully as possible the conditions that determine [the figure's] appearance and dictate [her] silence." Only for interpretive work where closure would be false.

## Check

- [ ] The opening earns the thesis; a reader who reads only the first two paragraphs can state the paper's claim.
- [ ] The closing introduces zero new claims, zero new evidence.
- [ ] Opening and closing are matched: a paradox opening gets a refusal-of-closure or mutual-illumination close, not a causal verdict; an inventory opening gets an analytical-completeness close, not a universal generalization.
- [ ] The results preview (if the genre uses one) matches the actual results section in order and number.

Confidence notes: paradox, vignette, and tableau openings are the best-grounded in the history lane (L2-E13's opening is genuine article text; L2-E2's paradox is abstract-explicit). Economics openings/closings are abstract-legible across L1. Infrastructural opening and canon-revision close rest on review summaries — marked as such.
