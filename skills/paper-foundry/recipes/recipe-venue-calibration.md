# Recipe: Venue Calibration

Different venues reward different architectures. Pick the claim architecture (recipe-contribution-claim) and the positioning dialect (recipe-lit-review-positioning) that the target venue's exemplars actually reward — not the one you prefer. When retargeting a paper, change the architecture, not just the citation format.

## Economics: top general-interest journals (QJE, AER)

Exemplars: L1-E12 (QJE 2024), L1-E3 (AER 2011, lane 2 but economics-genre).

- **Rewarded architecture:** identification-plus-construction or the four-stage evidentiary staircase (correlation → identification → falsification → mechanism). The argument's authority comes from the *design*, not the magnitude.
- **Gap move:** within-group comparison or mechanism-gap inside an established causal claim (a second-order gap — stronger than contesting the literature).
- **Evidence norm:** every claim on a named dataset and specification in the same paragraph; density is maximal.
- **Hedging:** causal verbs hedged lexically ("suggests," "consistent with"); design claims unhedged.
- **Positioning dialect:** mechanism slot — the paper is the next mechanism, the better identification. Predecessors slotted, not argued with.
- **What to change when retargeting here:** add the identification strategy; move the objection handling into the design (falsification battery); strip any universalizing close — end on the causal verdict.

## Economics: field and survey journals (JEL, JEP, RBPE, Social Science Quarterly, JEH)

Exemplars: L1-E5 (JEL 2022), L1-E13 (JEP 2022), L1-E9 (RBPE 2020), L1-E3 (RBPE 2010), L1-E8/L1-E11 (SSQ), L1-E7 (JEH 1974).

- **Rewarded architectures:** field-founding and legitimation-through-lineage (JEL); binary compare→crown and taxonomy-as-contribution (JEP); multi-method triangulation (RBPE); historical-anchor estimate and program-design checklist (SSQ); quantitative revision with steel-manned target (JEH).
- **Gap moves:** foundational-response, credit-and-vacate, benchmark-verdict, whether-to-how, revisionist debunking.
- **Evidence norm:** the abstract may carry the full argument skeleton (L1-E11's structured abstract IS the architecture) — these venues read abstracts as commitments.
- **Positioning dialect:** reclassification and method-lineage are welcome here; the literature may be reviewed by its own most-cited authors (L1-E13).
- **What to change when retargeting here:** the contribution can be second-order (a judging of methods); the verdict may be unhedged; the estimation menu can be detached from the policy meal.

## History: journals and monographs (AHR, Small Axe, university presses)

Exemplars: L2-E13 (AHR 2009), L2-E2 (Small Axe 2008), L2-E7/L2-E9/L2-E10/L2-E11/L2-E14 (monographs).

- **Rewarded architecture:** single-pivot thesis — one big reframing move, not stacked sub-claims. Staged process claims and paired-case connective claims for monographs.
- **Gap move:** paradox opening; archive-as-method; separation gap; inversion gap.
- **Evidence norm:** evidence inside the claim — vignette, emblematic figure, paired case. The cold open is a legitimate structure, not a gimmick.
- **Hedging:** architectural, not lexical — the claim is a re-description or a method, which bounds its own falsifiability.
- **Positioning dialect:** reframing, not refuting. Direct attacks on named predecessors are rare; the room is made by inversion, connection, or affiliation-extension.
- **Objection handling:** preemptive and early; the method answers the objection (L2-E2's confession; L2-E10's controlled reconstruction).
- **What to change when retargeting here:** collapse stacked sub-claims into one pivot; replace the sensitivity table with the emblematic scene; move the strongest objection into the introduction; let the closing refuse false closure if the evidence warrants it.

## Field and interdisciplinary journals (Du Bois Review, Social Science & Medicine, African Studies Review, Columbia Law Review)

Exemplars: L1-E6 (Du Bois Review), L1-E10 (Soc Sci & Medicine), L1-E4 (Columbia Law Review), L2-E12 (African Studies Review, metadata-only).

- **Rewarded architectures:** polemic-first → empirical adjudication; method-ladder with counterfactual legibility; taxonomy-as-contribution; programmatic field-definition (L2-E12 — the genre Foundry needs most; full text not yet acquired).
- **Gap moves:** discursive gap; intervention gap; explicit gap claim; design gap.
- **Positioning dialect:** against public narratives and debate stages, not just scholarship; author lists and vocabularies may signal cross-field import (L1-E10's economics apparatus inside epidemiology; L1-E8's transitional-justice vocabulary).
- **What to change when retargeting here:** the gap may be named against a methodological habit ("mere tallying") rather than a literature; the closing may universalize (particular diagnosis → universal prescription).

## Retargeting checklist

- [ ] Claim architecture swapped to the venue's rewarded form (not merely relabeled).
- [ ] Gap move swapped to the venue's idiom.
- [ ] Hedging norm swapped (lexical ↔ architectural).
- [ ] Positioning dialect swapped (mechanism slot ↔ reframing ↔ public-narrative).
- [ ] Closing move swapped to the venue's expected landing.
- [ ] The paper still makes the same substantive contribution — only the architecture moved.

Confidence notes: the economics-venue norms are the best-grounded in the corpus (one full text, multiple complete abstracts). History-venue norms rest on abstracts, one article opening, and publisher descriptions — the *shapes* are verified; their book-length execution is inferred. The African Studies Review programmatic-article norm (L2-E12) is metadata-only and flagged as the highest-priority acquisition.
