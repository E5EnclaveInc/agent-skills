# Recipe: Contribution-Claim Architecture

Pick ONE architecture for the paper's thesis. One paper, one architecture — blending two produces a paper that reads as two papers stapled together. Run recipe-venue-calibration before choosing: economics venues reward architectures 5-7; history venues reward 1-4.

## The architectures (each attested)

**1. Single-pivot thesis** (L2-E2, L2-E13, L2-E14, L2-E7): one big reframing move, not stacked sub-claims. "Social death" → "political life." Separate stories → linked stories. Europe-centered → Africa-shaped. The title itself often carries the pivot. Use when the contribution is conceptual. Hedging is built into the architecture (the claim is the *re-description*), not into the verbs.

**2. Stacked sub-claims, modular and redundant** (L1-E1): one big claim carried by stacked sub-claims where each block could carry the case alone — slavery era, Jim Crow era, modern era; measurement, accounting, policy. Design for redundancy: the argument must not depend on any single estimate or block holding up. Sequence the stacks chronologically or concrete-to-abstract.

**3. Multi-method triangulation / convergent bounds** (L1-E9): several independent methods, all landing in the same region, all dwarfing the benchmark. Sequence: benchmark first (open on the target), then each method with its number attached, then the convergence verdict. Display sensitivity explicitly (the 3%-vs-6% spread) — the sensitivity display IS the honesty mechanism; hiding it invites the "arbitrary rate" attack. Close by detaching estimation from verdict: claim the menu, not the meal.

**4. Binary compare → crown** (L1-E13): enumerate two families of approaches completely and neutrally (itemization vs. global indicator), then adjudicate and crown the winner with an unhedged verdict ("provides the best gauge"). The structure absorbs the arbitrariness objection by arguing for the *class* rather than the *figure*. Note: this is a second-order contribution (a judging of methods); it requires the literature to be mature enough to have methods worth judging. Also attested as taxonomy-as-contribution (L1-E4): the coverage is the contribution; end on analytical completeness, not a verdict.

**5. Method-ladder with counterfactual legibility** (L1-E10): single big claim executed as a ladder — (a) observed comparison, (b) back-calculated parameters, (c) simulated intervention. Keep the evidentiary status of each rung legible in the text: observed curves vs. modeled parameters vs. simulated effects must never blur. The contribution is a modeled world; say "would have" and "reasoning through," not "shows." Close with the universal-benefit generalization if the remedy's politics need it (particular diagnosis → universal prescription).

**6. Four-stage evidentiary staircase** (L1-E3, full text): (a) OLS/descriptive correlation, (b) causal identification (IV), (c) falsification tests, (d) mechanism decomposition. Preview all four stages in the introduction — the reader should know the staircase exists before climbing it. Each stage answers the objection the previous stage invites. This is the economics-journal genre structure; see recipe-venue-calibration.

**7. Identification-plus-construction** (L1-E12): the data construction is itself a contribution (linked census records 1850–1940 extended to 2000 neighborhoods and 2023 surnames), followed by the identification claim (RD design). Sequence description → mechanism → identification. The venue (top general-interest journal) is part of the architecture — file identification-credibility arguments where identification is the coin of the realm.

**8. Field-founding** (L1-E2, L1-E5): name the field and its object in the title; the contribution is the announcement of a literature. The 2005 paper does it raw; the 2022 JEL survey does it institutionalized — legitimation-through-lineage (nine named ancestors across disciplines), extension list as the closing move (past-justification → future-territory), JEL codes as quiet filing into the discipline's taxonomy. Use only when the body of work genuinely constitutes a field; otherwise it reads as branding.

**9. Staged process claim** (L2-E10): one big process claim sequenced as stages that are both places and transformations (Gold Coast shores → coastal forts → ship → market → reconstructed identity). Each stage is a place *and* a transformation. The narrative structure IS the claim architecture.

**10. Paired-case connective claim** (L2-E14): paradox → mechanism → mutual illumination. Name the causal arrows between the cases (Haiti → Cuban sugar boom → entrenchment); the payoff must be symmetric ("sheds fresh light on both") or the pairing reads as instrumental.

**11. Historical-anchor estimate** (L1-E8, L1-E13): a concrete historical counterfactual (the unfulfilled 1865 land promise) converted into a present-value foundation. Dual-use device: the anchor is both evidence (the promise existed) and method (the valuation basis). Compresses density; carries the "how" of program-design papers.

## Hedging norms (attested, venue-sensitive)

- Economics causal claims: lexical hedging is the norm ("arguably," "suggests," "consistent with") — L1-E3, full text. Method verdicts are unhedged ("provides the best gauge" — L1-E13).
- History reframing claims: hedging is architectural (the claim is a re-description or a method, which cannot be false in the way an estimate can) — L2-E2's writing "mimes" redress; it does not claim to achieve it.
- Estimation papers: hedge with explicit sensitivity ranges and named unfinished categories — L1-E9, L1-E11. The humility-about-novelty / boldness-about-results split (L1-E11) is the estimation sub-literature's signature: minimize the novelty claim, maximize the corrected number.
