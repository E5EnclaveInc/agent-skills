## Data-quality warnings (encode in any future use)

1. OpenAlex has no reliable standalone citation record for field-defining books: Gilroy's *The Black Atlantic* (1993), Patterson's *Slavery and Social Death* (1982), Hartman's *Scenes of Subjection* (1997), Rediker's *The Slave Ship* (2007), C. L. R. James's *The Black Jacobins*, Cedric Robinson's *Black Marxism* (1983). Book citations get merged into review records, inflating review counts.
2. One OpenAlex record for *The Black Jacobins* misattributes authorship ("Caron James, Pierre Naville") — banned from citation entirely.
3. Full-text acquisition was blocked by Cloudflare bot-challenges on all three attempted OA PDFs in lane 1 and on the Harvard DASH copy in lane 2; attempts were abandoned per policy, not bypassed. Only the NBER working-paper version of L2-E3 was legitimately obtained.
4. Author-name rendering differs cosmetically in OpenAlex ("Irā Berlin," "BRENT HAYES EDWARDS") — not counted as mismatches.
