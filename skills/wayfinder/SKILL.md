# Wayfinder

> **Attribution:** Adapted from mattpocock/skills (MIT). Upstream is canonical; this adaptation adds E5's curation and promotion-gate framing.
>
> Method only; no code or text copied. See `LICENSE` for the upstream MIT license notice.

Large efforts fail on undecided decisions, not on code. Map the decisions
first.

## Method

1. List every decision the effort contains — technical, scope, sequencing.
2. For each: options, what decides it (data, taste, authority), and who
   decides.
3. Order them: which decisions unblock which. Settle in that order.
4. Settled decisions are written down with date and rationale; they are
   not re-litigated without new evidence.

## Output

A decision map: the territory, the order of settling, and the log of what
was decided. Feeds to-spec.
