# State corporate registries

Florida is the proven lane. Other states follow the same pattern: every
Secretary of State (or Division of Corporations) runs a public business-entity
search with a detail page per entity and usually a document-number lookup.

## Florida (proven 2026-09-19)
- Search: https://search.sunbiz.org — Inquiry > search by Entity Name or
  Document Number.
- Detail page carries: status, document number, FEI/EIN (often NONE for new
  entities), date filed, effective date, entity type, registered agent,
  principal + mailing addresses, officers/directors with titles, annual
  report history.
- DBA / fictitious names are NOT on the entity detail page — Florida
  requires a separate fictitious name registration (searchable separately
  on Sunbiz).
- Annual reports due Jan 1 – May 1 each year beginning the year after filing;
  $400 late fee after May 1 (profit corps).

## Other states (unproven — adapt the brief, verify fields live)
- Pattern: `[state] secretary of state business entity search`.
- Record the same field set as Florida; note where a field doesn't exist
  (some states don't show EINs at all; some don't list officers).
- DBA handling varies by state: some show trade names on the entity page,
  most require a separate assumed-name filing.

## IRS lanes (federal, all states)
- Tax Exempt Organization Search: https://apps.irs.gov/app/eos
- Application status ("Where's My Application"):
  https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/where-is-my-exemption-application
  — shows which submission months are currently being processed. As of
  2026-09-19 the IRS was still on February 2026 submissions for 1024-A.
