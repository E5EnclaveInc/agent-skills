# Worked Example: A Playbook Brand's Quarter

Illustrative only — shows the city-map filled in end to end for a fictional
playbook brand, "Playbook Weekly" (practical AI playbooks for non-technical
small-business owners/operators; one playbook every Friday; archive free forever).

**Grand Central (the WHY):** AI is a practical tool for operators, not a project
for engineers.

---

**Destination 1 — Grow the newsletter into a self-sustaining engine**
- Metric: net new subscribers per week.
- Signal: "a small win you can copy this week."
- Passengers: non-technical small-business owners and operators who already feel
  behind on AI.
- Stops: 60-second "one playbook page, one win" videos; before/after operator
  stories.
- Timetable: 2 short videos/week.

**Destination 2 — Make the playbook archive the free growth engine**
- Metric: playbook-page visits → subscriber conversion rate.
- Signal: "the library is free forever; one playbook every Friday."
- Passengers: curious operators browsing for free help; referrers looking for a
  link worth sharing.
- Stops: Friday playbook-release posts; archive roundup threads ("5 playbooks
  that saved owners an hour a week").
- Timetable: 1 release post + 1 roundup/week.

**Destination 3 — Convert trust into revenue (audit / kit / membership)**
- Metric: audit bookings.
- Signal: "we'll find what you're missing in 30 minutes."
- Passengers: engaged subscribers who have consumed several playbooks and trust
  the brand.
- Stops: live teardown clips; operator testimonials.
- Timetable: 1 teardown clip/week.

---

**Balance:** Destinations 1 and 2 are growth; Destination 3 is loyalty/conversion.
Each is judged on its own metric — a teardown clip is never graded on subscriber
growth, and a roundup thread is never graded on audit bookings.

**Review:** monthly check of each line's metric; full map redone next quarter.
