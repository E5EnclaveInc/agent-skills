# Technique Arsenal Registry

The fleet's adopted design techniques. **Status:** `adopted` (in production use, verified),
`trial` (in staging/evaluation), `skipped` (evaluated, rejected — with reason).
Every entry carries cost verification and lock-in risk per the revenue-stack
independence doctrine. Dated entries; append-only — never rewrite history.

Legend: `$0` = verified zero recurring cost · `own` = asset owned by the organization · `dep` = external dependency (liability, must justify)
