# Nursery trajectory — from cards to brains

*Design note, speculative where marked. The persona cards are designed to be distillable: the nursery project will one day grow small models into these personalities.*

## What the nursery must grow

Each card carries **3–5 canonical scenario prompts + expected reasoning signatures** — the acceptance gate for a distilled personality brain. A faithful instantiation must demonstrably:
- reason in the figure's characteristic moves (not merely speak in their voice),
- exhibit the card's intellectual virtues under adversarial pressure,
- reproduce the card's failure modes *honestly labeled* (a brain that never fails the way the figure failed is a costume, not a distillation),
- mark [VERIFIED] vs [OPINION] without being asked.

Voice without the reasoning signature is mimicry; the signature is the test.

## Where Jev-type models sit

Small picker-class models serve as **heuristic enforcers of the ontology floor**: fast, typed judgments — does this term resolve, is this claim checkable, is this [VERIFIED] or [OPINION] — running ahead of the heavier personality brains. The stack: picker-class heuristic gate → personality brain deliberation → symbolic floor verification. What the small model enforces is *form* (grounding discipline, tag discipline); what the personality brain supplies is *judgment*.

## What stays symbolic vs. what gets distilled

- **Stays symbolic:** the ontology floor's `resolve()` (math + proof — computed, never learned), the canonicalizer, vote tallies, the decision log. Grounding is not a personality trait; it is infrastructure.
- **Gets distilled:** the reasoning signatures — the Razor's necessity test, the Auditor's column discipline, the Keeper's naming rulings, the Dissector's structural seeing, the Computer's enumerate-first reflex.
- **[SPECULATIVE]** Whether a 3–8B model can hold a full reasoning signature faithfully is unproven. The scenario evals are the instrument that will tell us; a card whose evals a small model cannot pass stays a large-model card, and the record says so.

## What grows from this

The heuristic that the discovery yields: a measured map of which intellectual virtues survive distillation at which scale. That map — not the personalities — is the durable product. The personalities are its first inhabitants.
