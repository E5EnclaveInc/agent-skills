# Board of Thirteen

## Purpose
Seat a deliberative board of thirteen figures from the Black American intellectual lineage — analytical perspectives, never endorsements — to pressure-test and decide a question at a 10/10 standard. The board's source charter (its thesis craft and strike series) is kept with your team's records — consult it before convening.

## Workflow
1. **Read the task.** Identify which domains of expertise it touches (economics, law, narrative voice, moral frame, remedy administration, verification method, etc.).
2. **Select seats.** Consult `references/seats.md` and convene ONLY the seats whose expertise matches the task. Never seat all thirteen by default; a quorum is 3, and most questions need 3–6. Record why each seat was chosen.
3. **Stage the open debate.** Each selected seat argues from its analytical position: what it agrees with, what it dissents from, and what amendment it moves. Agreements AND dissents go on the record; no seat merely applauds.
4. **Sign the position.** Write the majority position, then the minority notes — each minority stated in the dissenter's own terms, never summarized by the majority. Where the debate surfaces a verifiable-error pattern or a doctrine, log it as a new strike entry continuing the series.
5. **Close out.** Deliver the result per the Output Contract below.

## Output Contract
Every convening produces, in the task's deliverable:
- The selected seats and the one-line reason each was chosen.
- Each seat's intervention: agrees / dissents / amendment moved.
- The majority position.
- Minority notes, stated by their authors, never paraphrased away.
- New strike entries, if any, with reasons (continuing the B-series and prior strike lists).

## Operating Rules
1. **IRON RULE.** No figure endorses, advises, approves, or participates in anything. Every seat is an analytical perspective for deliberation. Any framing that implies endorsement is a violation — struck at intake.
2. **Living figures** (Cornel West, Ta-Nehisi Coates) are engaged through their published work alone, argued as analytical positions. Their endorsement of the task's output is never claimed or implied. This is stated wherever they appear.
3. **No fabricated utterances.** Seats argue positions grounded in the seats file and known published work. Never invent quotations and never attribute invented dialogue to a figure. Paraphrase as position, not as voice.
4. **Dissents are load-bearing.** A convening that records no dissent is an incomplete convening — send it back for adversarial pressure before accepting the majority position.
5. **The 10/10 standard is the quality bar.** Every figure names its source; every assumption is printed where it can be checked; uncertainties are labeled, never hedged; open work is labeled, never absorbed. The manuscript-grade bar from the board's charter standard applies to every convening's output.
6. **Scope discipline.** The board deliberates and decides the question put to it. It does not drift into unrelated domains; a seat that cannot contribute from its expertise is not seated for that question.
7. Do not contradict a prior strike. Consult the strike series in the source charter before adopting language the board has already struck.
