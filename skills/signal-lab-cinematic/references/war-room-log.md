# War Room Log — a Corporate-Site Signal Lab (2026)

The canonical worked example behind this skill. An agent picking up `signal-lab-cinematic` should read this to understand where the rules came from — the rules are settlements, not opinions.

## The brief

The founder liked a sibling brand's site (cinematic, dawn-film register) but said the corporate site "looks more like a landing page." Directive: raise the corporate site to that caliber **without making a carbon copy**, bring the technical diagrams to life, add a dynamic cinematic hero — grounded in the team's attention-science, retention, media-company, and packaging doctrine.

## The war room

Four perspectives convened per the team's dispatch doctrine: cinematic designer, attention-science strategist, diagram-to-film engineer, red team. Consensus: **"The Signal Lab."**

Two genuine conflicts were resolved on the record:

1. **Hero film vs. no film.** A 4.4MB golden-hour aerial video existed and was approved — but it violated the fleet's 2.5MB/10s canon, was hostile to the low-bandwidth audience the company exists to serve, and *dawn film is the sibling brand's signature*, creating entity-blur between the two organizations. Consensus: the video does not ship in its current form. The hero becomes cinematic through a lightweight canvas instrument layer over the poster.
2. **Timecodes.** The engineer proposed `SIM T+` timecodes as an honesty device; the red team banned all timecode grammar as documentary grammar implying recorded events. Consensus: no timecodes. Instrument telemetry stamps (`FRAME 0642 · LINK −3 dB`) carry the same cinematic charge without implying a recording.

## What the red team killed

- The 4.4MB hero video in its current form.
- Looping ambient motion, "LIVE" grammar, pulsing status beacons, ticking counters.
- Documentary-style timecodes.
- A falsifiable "0 third parties" stat (collapses in View Source against Turnstile).
- Present-tense capability copy ("The mesh heals itself" → "is designed to heal").
- Any motion whose honesty label lived only in surrounding copy.

## The drift template

An earlier iteration (preserved in a directory tagged `DO_NOT_SHIP.md`, originals untouched) is the negative example: present-tense copy drift, a pulsing "live" beacon, 4.4MB dual-element autoplay video, deleted federal identifiers, a falsifiable third-party stat while loading Turnstile. The skill's guards exist so these mistakes never return.

## The concept that survived

The sibling brand makes the visitor *feel the dawn*; the corporate site makes the visitor *read the field like an instrument*. If an animation can't carry a label, it doesn't ship. The metric was pre-committed before code: ≥20% of sessions reaching the evidence-ledger chapter take one trust action within 90 days, with falsification thresholds that distinguish attention failure from trust failure from motion-carried numbers.
