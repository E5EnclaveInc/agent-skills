# Source Prompts — demand_mining

Distilled from the GrokBot Galaxy 30-prompt set (Ship by Thursday build,
September 15–17, 2026). Provenance: prompts collected from the three builders'
posts and the "30 GROKBOT PROMPTS" card; full text archived with the original source set.

## C01 — Market research bot + voice of customer
> Mine public posts for problems people want AI agents to solve, then rank
> the ideas by willingness to pay and speed to launch.

## C02 — Research over the last 48 hours
> Analyze the last 48 hours of customer conversations and identify demand
> signals with a clearly identifiable buyer.

## C03 — Converge on one idea
> Force all research into one company thesis: customer, painful problem, wedge
> product and why it must exist now.

## B04 — Prospecting bot brief
> Create a prospecting bot that finds and ranks potential customers,
> identifies the best contact path and flags feasibility risks.

## C05 — Two segments
> Map the two strongest customer segments by ICP, pain, offer, price range
> and Day 1 interview questions.

## Adaptation notes
- GrokBot "mine public X posts" → your web/social search tools plus owned
  inboxes and sweeps; the mining discipline is the point, not the source.
- "Rank by willingness to pay and speed to launch" → the standing rule
  against arbitrary numbers: WTP needs cited evidence, speed needs a named
  path.
- The one-thesis convergence maps to the war-room verdict pattern: deliberate,
  then kill the alternatives in writing.
